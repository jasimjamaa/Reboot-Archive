Our approach

Useful truth, down to the small things.

Older devices have lived a life already. We check the functions that matter for each item, inspect its condition closely, and make the results part of the listing.

The listing process

Three careful passes.

What we can test depends on the device, its age, and the accessories available. The listing describes the checks and results that apply to that individual piece.

01   IDENTIFY

Know what it is

We check the model and identifying details, then note the key specifications and accessories included with the item.

02   TRY THE FUNCTIONS

Put it through its paces

We try the relevant functions and controls for the device. Where applicable, we check power, charging, playback, buttons, ports, and battery performance.

03   INSPECT & RECORD

Show its true condition

We look over the item for wear, marks, and damage, then photograph and describe the details that help you understand its condition.

A note on vintage

Tested is a snapshot in time.

Many archive pieces are decades old. A device that works today can still show age, and its battery life may differ from a new product. We describe what we observe during testing and call out known limitations in the listing.

Checks vary from one model to another. If a function cannot be checked, or a part is missing, the listing should say so. Please use the item’s photographs, condition notes, and included-items section as the final guide to that specific piece.

No two histories are quite the same. The listing tells the story of this one.

How results are rated

A clear rating for every check.

Each applicable check receives one of these ratings, so you can see at a glance what condition a feature or area is in.

  • ImmaculateExcellent condition
  • Light DamageMinor signs of wear or damage
  • Normal DamageTypical visible wear for its age
  • Heavy DamageSignificant wear or damage
  • Not FunctionalDoes not work as intended
  • UntestedCould not be verified
  • InapplicableDoes not apply to this device
Repairs & transparency

Any work or issues, stated plainly.

When needed, we repair some parts to make a device usable again. We explicitly state known product issues and relevant repairs in the listing, alongside the test results, photographs, and condition notes.

You should know what was checked, what was repaired, and what still needs attention.
