About this record

Release dates refer to first availability in the United States. Prices are original US starting prices where documented and may reflect carrier contracts for early iPhones. Display sizes and original iOS versions are model-specific; “varies” marks families whose members differed. Later products are included only where announced by Apple.

Primary references: Apple Newsroom iPhone archive, Apple product announcements and Apple technical specifications. For Historical product images use fixed, per-model records. Image and source links are intentionally left blank for archive curation.

Latest verified catalogue endpoint: iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo, announced September 9, 2026. The current Apple Newsroom archive lists both announcements and the Duo’s October 23 availability.
