2001

Scope and archival notes

This timeline counts each separately marketed generation and meaningful hardware revision once. Capacity and colour options appear as variants within that model; they are not duplicated as separate generations. U2 models and the Harry Potter engraved iPod are shown as special-edition entries because Apple documented those offers. (PRODUCT)RED availability is listed under the corresponding model. Apple’s personalised laser engraving service could put customer-selected text on otherwise standard iPods; custom engravings and third-party modifications are not treated as Apple special editions.

Dates distinguish announcement from first availability where sources report both. Prices are original US launch prices and can differ by configuration. Early firmware versions were not always consistently recorded in Apple’s public documents; “varies / not stated” is used where appropriate. Bundled accessories also differed across markets and model revisions.

Sources: Apple Support: Identify your iPod model; Apple Newsroom iPod archive; EveryMac / EveryiPod technical catalogue; Apple’s original iPod announcement and individual announcements for shuffle, nano, touch, classic, U2 edition, and (PRODUCT)RED. The HP-branded releases are corroborated by Hewlett-Packard History Archives and Macworld’s HP photo iPod report. Apple Support lists the Harry Potter 20 GB iPod manual. Product thumbnails are fetched lazily from Wikimedia Commons using a model-specific file search; a matching photograph may be unavailable for some revisions, and the page does not substitute another generation’s image.

Catalogue includes 31 standard generation/revision entries plus 8 identifiable special-edition or partner-branded entries: four U2 models, two HP-branded releases, the Harry Potter engraved edition, and the first (PRODUCT)RED nano. Later (PRODUCT)RED colourways are listed under each compatible model rather than counted as additional hardware generations. Custom engravings and third-party modifications are excluded.
